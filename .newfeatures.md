## 1. Executive Summary & Paradigm Shift

The **Adaptive Intelligence Engine (AIE)** is an autonomous, psychometrically grounded educational engine designed for high-stakes competitive examinations in India: **JEE (Engineering)**, **NEET (Medical)**, and **UPSC (Civil Services)**. 

Traditional EdTech platforms rely on static question banks, uniform homework assignments, and linear progression bars. In contrast, this engine models the student's cognitive state as a dynamic probability distribution across a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of prerequisite concepts. 

The latest evolutionary leap integrates:
1. **Live External AI Repositories**: On-demand streaming from the HuggingFace `169Pi/exambench` dataset (405,906 competitive reasoning items) and `Reja1/jee-neet-benchmark` (official 2024–2025 question crops).
2. **Interleaved Multi-Subject Daily Assignment Subsystem**: Automatic daily generation of 60–75 question problem sets distributed across the 3 canonical subjects of the student’s active exam stream, incorporating cognitive load balancing, auto-save state persistence, and automatic feedback loops into Bayesian Knowledge Tracing.
3. **UPSC Civil Services Suite**: Dual-tier preparation encompassing Prelims general studies testing and Mains analytical essay evaluation with automated 5-dimensional rubric scoring.
4. **Triple-Role Access & HUD Buffer**: Role segregation (Student, Guest, Admin) wrapped in an ultra-low latency responsive interface with live mathematical canvas rendering.

## 3. Core Pedagogical & Mathematical Foundations

### 3.1 Multi-Factor Mastery Formulation
Mastery of a concept $c$ at time $t$ is not merely an average of past test scores. It is computed as a weighted composite:

$$M(c, t) = w_{rec} \cdot A_{rec}(c) + w_{bkt} \cdot P(L_{c,t}) + w_{cov} \cdot C_{cov}(c) + w_{decay} \cdot R(c, t)$$

Where:
- $A_{rec}(c) \in [0, 1]$: Exponentially smoothed accuracy over the last $K$ attempts ($K=5$).
- $P(L_{c,t}) \in [0, 1]$: Posterior probability of concept acquisition from Bayesian Knowledge Tracing.
- $C_{cov}(c) \in [0, 1]$: Curriculum coverage ratio (ratio of attempted vs. available items in the topic tier).
- $R(c, t) \in [0, 1]$: Ebbinghaus retention probability based on time elapsed since last retrieval practice.
- Calibrated weights: $w_{rec} = 0.35$, $w_{bkt} = 0.30$, $w_{cov} = 0.15$, $w_{decay} = 0.20$ ($\sum w = 1.0$).

### 3.2 Ebbinghaus Memory Decay & Spaced Repetition
Human memory decays exponentially in the absence of active retrieval. The retention probability $R(t)$ after elapsed time $t$ (in days) is formulated as:

$$R(t) = e^{-\frac{t}{S}}$$

Where $S$ is the **Memory Stability Factor**:

$$S_{new} = S_{prior} \cdot \left(1 + \alpha \cdot M(c) \cdot (1 - \beta)^{\text{failed\_reviews}}\right)$$

- $\alpha = 1.618$ (Golden ratio multiplier for successful retrieval).
- $\beta = 0.40$ (Penalty multiplier for failed recall).
- When $R(t) < 0.60$, the concept is automatically queued into the **Spaced Repetition Review Queue**.

### 3.3 Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT)
For each concept node, student mastery state transitions between *Unlearned* ($L_0$) and *Learned* ($L_1$):
- $P(L_0)$: Initial prior knowledge (default 0.15 for beginners, 0.40 for diagnostic passers).
- $P(T)$: Probability of transition from unlearned to learned state given an opportunity ($T = 0.18$).
- $P(G)$: Guess probability (student answers correctly despite not knowing the concept, $G = 0.20$ for 4-option MCQs).
- $P(S)$: Slip probability (student knows the concept but makes a careless slip, $S = 0.08$).

Upon receiving observation $Y_t \in \{1 \text{ (correct)}, 0 \text{ (incorrect)}\}$:

$$P(L_t | Y_t = 1) = \frac{P(L_{t-1}) \cdot (1 - P(S))}{P(L_{t-1}) \cdot (1 - P(S)) + (1 - P(L_{t-1})) \cdot P(G)}$$

$$P(L_t | Y_t = 0) = \frac{P(L_{t-1}) \cdot P(S)}{P(L_{t-1}) \cdot P(S) + (1 - P(L_{t-1})) \cdot (1 - P(G))}$$

Posterior update prior to next opportunity:

$$P(L_{t+1}) = P(L_t | Y_t) + \left(1 - P(L_t | Y_t)\right) \cdot P(T)$$

### 3.4 2-Parameter Logistic Item Response Theory (IRT 2PL)
The probability of a student with latent ability $\theta \in [-3.0, +3.0]$ correctly solving question $i$ with difficulty $b_i$ and discrimination $a_i$ is:

$$P(Y_i = 1 | \theta, a_i, b_i) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-D \cdot a_i (\theta - b_i)}}$$

Where:
- $D = 1.702$ (scaling factor to match the normal ogive).
- $b_i \in [-2.5, +2.5]$: Item difficulty parameter.
- $a_i \in [0.5, 2.5]$: Item discrimination parameter (slope of the item characteristic curve).
- The engine dynamically estimates student ability $\hat{\theta}$ via Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) or Expected A Posteriori (EAP) following each assessment session.

### 3.5 NetworkX Prerequisite DAG & Topological Curricula
The curriculum is formalized as a directed acyclic graph $G = (V, E)$ where vertices $v \in V$ represent atomic concepts and directed edges $(u, v) \in E$ indicate that concept $u$ is a strict prerequisite for concept $v$.
- **Unlock Rule**: A concept $v$ is unlocked if and only if:
  $$\forall u \in \text{Parents}(v), \quad M(u) \ge \tau_{\text{mastery}} \quad (\tau_{\text{mastery}} = 0.70)$$
- **Roadmap Sequence**: Computed via topological sort with tie-breaking based on exam weightage coefficients ($W_{exam}$).

### 4.1 HuggingFace `169Pi/exambench` 405k Question Stream
- **Endpoint**: `https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=169Pi%2Fexambench&config=default&split=train`
- **Data Attributes**: Each raw record contains `prompt` (problem statement), `response` (solution text), and `complex_cot` (Chain-of-Thought formal step-by-step mathematical or scientific derivation).
- **Stream Classification Logic**:
  `ExamBenchService.classify_row()` performs deterministic multi-label keyword extraction:
  - **Mathematics (JEE)**: Vector calculus, differential equations, matrices, coordinate geometry, conic sections, limits, polynomials.
  - **Physics (JEE/NEET)**: Maxwell equations, Lorentz forces, rotational dynamics, thermodynamics, simple harmonic motion, optics, semiconductors.
  - **Chemistry (JEE/NEET)**: Coordination complexes, reaction kinetics, hybridization, Gibbs free energy, electrochemistry, organic synthesis.
  - **Biology (NEET)**: Cellular biology, Mendelian genetics, plant physiology, human endocrine and neural systems, ecology, biotechnology.
  - **General Studies (UPSC)**: Constitutional governance, public policy, sustainable development, environmental economics, administrative ethics.

### 4.2 HuggingFace `Reja1/jee-neet-benchmark` Official Question Crops
- **Endpoint**: `https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=Reja1%2Fjee-neet-benchmark&config=default&split=test`
- **Capabilities**: Directly imports authentic question paper crops from actual 2024 and 2025 JEE Advanced and NEET-UG tests.
- **Image Preservation**: Ingests high-resolution visual crop links directly into `Question.image_url`.
- **Key Normalization**: Parses ambiguous key structures (`"1"`, `["C"]`, `"Option 2"`) into normalized canonical options (`A, B, C, D`).

### 4.3 Resilience, Local Cache Hierarchy & Offline Failover
1. **Tier 1 (Memory)**: Runtime LRU object cache for active assessment sessions.
2. **Tier 2 (Disk File Cache)**: `data/exambench_cache.json` and `data/jee_neet_benchmark_cache.json`. When the live network request to HuggingFace exceeds 15 seconds or drops, the service falls back to the disk cache without interrupting the student.
3. **Tier 3 (Relational Database)**: Persisted in SQLite with relational integrity linking `Question` $\to$ `Concept` $\to$ `Topic` $\to$ `Chapter` $\to$ `Subject` $\to$ `Exam`.

## 5. The Daily 3-Subject Assignment Engine: Addon Blueprint

### 5.1 Cognitive Architecture: Interleaved vs. Blocked Practice
Educational psychology demonstrates that **blocked practice** (studying only Physics for 4 hours) produces short-term fluency but poor long-term retention. In contrast, **interleaved practice** (alternating between Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology) introduces **desirable difficulties** (Bjork & Bjork), forcing the brain to repeatedly reconstruct retrieval pathways for different cognitive domains.

The Assignment Engine automatically provisions a daily 3-subject set customized to the student's exam stream:
- **JEE Main**: Physics (20–25 Qs) + Chemistry (20–25 Qs) + Mathematics (20–25 Qs). Total: **60–75 Questions/day**.
- **NEET-UG**: Biology (20–25 Qs) + Physics (20–25 Qs) + Chemistry (20–25 Qs). Total: **60–75 Questions/day**.
- **UPSC Civil Services**: General Studies (20–25 Qs) + Science & Tech (20–25 Qs) + CSAT Quantitative/Logical (20–25 Qs). Total: **60–75 Questions/day**.

### 5.2 Dynamic Item Sampling & Ability-Difficulty Calibration
When `/assignments/today/{student_id}` is triggered, the engine executes the following assembly pipeline:

### 5.3 Distractor Synthesis & Cognitive Error Modeling
When questions are synthesized from raw ExamBench reasoning rows, the correct answer is extracted from the solution derivation, while **distractors are systematically engineered to embody predictable cognitive error types**:

1. **Calculation Error (`CALCULATION_ERROR`)**:
   - Inversion of boundary scaling factor ($k \to 1/k$, multiplying by $2$ instead of dividing).
   - Sign flipping in thermodynamic work or electric potential.
2. **Conceptual Misconception (`CONCEPTUAL_ERROR`)**:
   - Assuming a parameter remains constant when a flux or temperature variation exists.
   - Confusing dominant vs. recessive phenotypic ratios in dihybrid crosses.
3. **Formula Selection Error (`FORMULA_SELECTION_ERROR`)**:
   - Applying a linear static approximation to a non-linear dynamic system.
   - Using kinematic constant-acceleration equations in non-uniform acceleration fields.
4. **Reading / Comprehension Slip (`READING_ERROR`)**:
   - Overlooking qualifiers such as *"which of the following is INCORRECT"*.

### 5.5 Hint Reveal Logic & Penalty Propagation
The assignment interface supports multi-tier progressive hinting:
- **Level 1 Hint (Concept Identification)**: Outlines the governing physical law or theorem. Penalty: $-5\%$ item score.
- **Level 2 Hint (Formula / Equation Clue)**: Identifies the algebraic formula to apply. Penalty: $-15\%$ item score.
- **Level 3 Hint (Step 1 Computation)**: Shows initial substitution of boundary parameters. Penalty: $-30\%$ item score.
- Penalties propagate into the question's effective accuracy before computing mastery updates, preserving pedagogical accountability.

## 6. UPSC Civil Services Subsystem

The UPSC engine provides dual-mode preparation tailored to the Union Public Service Commission examination scheme:

### 6.1 Prelims MCQ Engine
- **Objective Testing**: Multi-statement MCQs covering Constitutional Polity, Modern Indian History, Geography & Environment, Macroeconomics, and Science & Technology.
- **Negative Marking Calculation**: Standard $+2.0$ marks for correct answers and $-0.66$ marks for incorrect selections.
- **Elimination Technique Explanations**: Solutions emphasize test-taking strategy (e.g., extreme word traps like *"always"*, *"completely"*).

### 6.2 Mains Analytical Answer Workspace & Multi-Dimensional Rubric
Mains preparation requires descriptive, balanced, policy-grounded writing under strict time and word limits.
- **Interactive Workspace**: Split-pane interface featuring official authentic UPSC prompts (GS Papers 1, 2, 3, 4), countdown timer, dynamic word counter with progress rings, and structured answer input.
- **Multi-Dimensional AI Rubric**:
  When submitted, the answer is evaluated against 5 rigorous parameters:
  1. **Understanding & Relevance (Max 3.0 pts)**: Direct engagement with directive verbs (*Critically Examine*, *Discuss*, *Elucidate*).
  2. **Structure & Organization (Max 2.0 pts)**: Presence of crisp Introduction, thematic sub-headings, bulleted arguments, and forward-looking Conclusion.
  3. **Content Depth & Facts (Max 2.5 pts)**: Inclusion of relevant Articles of the Constitution, Supreme Court judgments (e.g., *Kesavananda Bharati*, *Puttaswamy*), committee reports (e.g., *Nolan*, *Sarkaria*), and economic indices.
  4. **Policy & Constitutional Alignment (Max 1.5 pts)**: Alignment with constitutional morality, directive principles, and national development goals.
  5. **Critical Balance & Multi-perspectivity (Max 1.0 pt)**: Objective synthesis weighing benefits against structural challenges.
- **Instant Rubric Breakdown**: Generates qualitative feedback, dimensions hit/missed, and scaled percentage scores.

## 8. API Specifications & Communication Contracts

### 8.1 Daily Assignments (`/api/v1/assignments`)
- `GET /today/{student_id}?questions_per_subject=20`:
  Returns today’s 3-subject problem set. If ungenerated, assembles questions across stream subjects and returns complete JSON with subject tabs and options.
- `POST /save-progress`:
  Body: `{ assignment_id: string, responses: [{ question_id, student_answer, is_marked_review, time_taken_seconds }] }`
  Autosaves intermediate progress without terminating the attempt.
- `POST /submit`:
  Body: `{ assignment_id: string, responses: [...], time_taken_seconds: int }`
  Evaluates all items, logs errors, updates BKT/Mastery, records streak, and returns final percentage and per-subject breakdown.
- `GET /history/{student_id}`:
  Returns assignment completion log and calculated daily practice streak.

### 8.2 External Benchmarks & Live Seeds (`/api/v1/curriculum`)
- `GET /exambench/live-sample?offset=0&length=10`:
  Fetches and synthesizes real-time MCQs directly from the Hugging Face `169Pi/exambench` dataset.
- `POST /exambench/seed-database?count=50`:
  Persists live Hugging Face questions into the local SQLite database.
- `GET /benchmark/live-sample?offset=0&length=10`:
  Retrieves official JEE Advanced & NEET question paper crops from `Reja1/jee-neet-benchmark`.

### 8.3 UPSC Civil Services Subsystem (`/api/v1/upsc`)
- `GET /mains-prompts`:
  Retrieves curated UPSC GS 1–4 analytical descriptive questions.
- `GET /prelims-quiz`:
  Returns multi-choice Prelims practice questions.
- `POST /evaluate-written`:
  Body: `{ student_id, question_id, answer_text, time_taken_seconds }`
  Grades written answer against the 5-point rubric and returns dimension scores.
- `GET /history/{student_id}`:
  Fetches past descriptive submissions and evaluations.

## 10. Verification, Latency Budgets & Security Model

### 10.1 Latency Budgets
- Static Asset Serving: `< 15ms` (local caching & minified CSS/JS).
- Local SQLite Read (Dashboard / Roadmap): `< 35ms`.
- Assignment Generation & IRT Calibration: `< 120ms`.
- Hugging Face Datasets Server Remote Query: `< 1800ms` (guarded by a 15s timeout with immediate fallback to disk cache).

### 10.2 Security & Admin Model
- **Student Authentication**: Bearer tokens and session keys stored in `localStorage` with client-side role validation.
- **Admin Access Control**:
  Protected by administrative header validation:
  `X-Admin-Key: aie_internal_2024` or `X-Admin-Key: 1234admin`.
  Restricted actions include `/api/v1/admin/reset-db`, `/api/v1/curriculum/exambench/seed-database`, and system telemetry extraction.
- **Foreign Key Integrity**:
  All quiz attempt sessions and assignment submissions automatically verify student record persistence prior to inserting child attempts, eliminating SQLite `IntegrityError` faults.
